How the index works

Each component is turned into a percentile (0–100) across all districts, then weighted: density 30% (rooftop + balcony systems per 1,000 inhabitants), growth rate 30% (new systems per 1,000 inhabitants in 12 months), volume 30% (absolute new systems in 12 months) and battery share 10%. With equal weights the ranking barely changes (Spearman ρ = ), so the result does not hinge on the chosen weights. Large ground-mounted solar parks are left out of the density measure because they usually connect at higher voltage levels.
